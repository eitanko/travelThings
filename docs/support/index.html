Travel Things Support

Travel Things is an offline-first travel document wallet for iOS: save tickets, bookings, passports and visas, and find them again instantly.

Contact

Found a bug or have a question or suggestion? Open an issue on GitHub.

FAQ

How do I add a document?

Tap add, then choose a file, pick a photo, or scan a paper document with the camera. You can also use Share from another app and pick Travel Things.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Will I lose documents if I delete the app?

Yes. Documents are stored only on your device (and in your own device backups, if enabled), so export anything important before deleting the app.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, the app works fully offline.

Camera or photo access is not working

Open iOS Settings → Travel Things and make sure Camera and Photos access are enabled.
